History of Present Illness
Summary: A man with fever and rash after travel to Africa.
A man in his fifties, approximately 2.5 weeks before evaluation, had spent time in the grasslands of South Africa, including lying on the ground observing animals. Afterwards, he noticed multiple bites on his thighs and arms. Ten days later, he developed a "black spot" on his right upper thigh and, three days later, developed acute fever, shaking chills, night sweats, and myalgias.
Past Medical History
He had a history of cataracts and duodenal ulcer disease.
Epidemiological History
He had recently traveled, as above.
Physical Examination
There was a necrotic lesion (2 centimeters in diameter) on the right upper thigh, with tender right inguinal adenopathy and several scattered papulovesicular lesions over the back, arms and legs (Figures 1 and 2).

Studies
The white blood cell count and differential were normal.
Diagnostic Procedure(s) and Result(s)
Acute and convalescent serology demonstrated a rise in titer to the spotted fever group of Rickettsia, from less than 1:16 to positive at 1:512 dilution.
Treatment and Followup
Discussion
Final Diagnosis
South African Tick Bite Fever, due to the spotted fever group of Rickettsia.
