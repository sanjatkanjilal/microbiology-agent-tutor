History of Present Illness
Summary: A man from Brazil with a rash of several years' duration.
A Brazilian man in his sixties was urged by a relative to have his rash of several years' duration evaluated. The lesions were mildly dysesthetic. He reported no fevers, chills, weight loss or other symptoms.
Past Medical History
Non-contributory.
Epidemiological History
The patient had recently immigrated to the United States from Brazil to live with his relatives.
Physical Examination
He appeared well and was afebrile. The examination revealed a rash (shown in Figures 1 and 2). An area in the center of the rash had mildly decreased sensation.
Figure 1. Physical findings.
Figure 2. Physical findings.
Diagnostic Procedure(s) and Result(s)
A skin biopsy was performed and revealed a perivascular lymphocytic infiltrate with multinucleated giant cells (Figure 3) consistent with borderline tuberculoid leprosy. Special stains, including acid-fast staining, were all negative.
Figure 3. Skin biopsy results.
Treatment and Followup
He was successfully treated with dapsone and rifampin.
Discussion
The physical findings are consistent with Tinea, however the duration of symptoms and biopsy results are not. Cutaneous leishmaniasis typically presents as a papule that subsequently ulcerates. Psoriasis typically occurs on extensor surfaces and has more scales. The rash of secondary syphilis does not last years. The history and biopsy results are diagnostic of borderline tuberculoid leprosy.
Final Diagnosis
Borderline tuberculoid leprosy, caused by Mycobacterium leprae.
