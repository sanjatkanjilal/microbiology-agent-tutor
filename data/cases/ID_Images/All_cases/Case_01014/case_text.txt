History of Present Illness
IDSA 2001 Summary: An infant with a lesion on the nose.
A 5-month-old infant developed small red papules on the nose at three-and-one-half months of age. Three weeks later, the child developed a scab over the lesions. Mupirocin ointment treatment was used with little response.
Fluormetholone ophthalmic ointment and oral cephalexin were given without response.
Epidemiological History
The patient lived in a city in the Western United States. There was no history of travel or animal contact. The patient had gone to a pumpkin patch 3 weeks after the onset of the lesion and sat in a haystack on the mother’s lap.
Physical Examination
The examination was normal except for the lesion on the nose as shown in Figures 1 and 2.
Figure 1. Physical findings.
Figure 2. Physical findings.
Diagnostic Procedure(s) and Result(s)
Potassium hydroxide (KOH) scraping was negative. Culture of the lesion grew Sporothrix schenckii.
Treatment and Followup
The patient was treated with itraconazole for four months. The child's appearance one month after the completion of therapy is shown in Figure 3.
Figure 3. Physical findings at follow-up.
Discussion
Sporothrix schenckii is a dimorphic fungus that is ubiquitous and can be isolated from soil, plants, and hay. Sporotrichosis most commonly presents as a cutaneous infection at a site of minor trauma after an average incubation period of 7-30 days.
Final Diagnosis
Sporothrix schenckii infection in an infant.
