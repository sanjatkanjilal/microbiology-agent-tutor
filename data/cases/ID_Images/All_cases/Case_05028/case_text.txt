Final Diagnosis
Streptococcus Group B, in cerebrospinal fluid.
A gram stain of cerebrospinal fluid shows round gram positive organisms in chains, clusters and pairs. The appearance suggested the possibility of Streptococcus Group A or B, both of which tend to be more rounded and in chains, or Streptococcus pneumoniae, which tends to be in pairs. The organism was later identified to be Streptococcus Group B.
