History of Present Illness
Summary: A woman with an abnormal colonoscopy.
A nursing home resident in her forties was referred for colonoscopy to evaluate guaiac positive stools.
Past Medical History
There was a history of intellectual disability.
Medications
None.
Epidemiological History
The patient was a nursing home resident in an urban area in New England.
Studies
Results of a complete blood count and serum levels of electrolytes, urea nitrogen, creatinine, glucose, and liver function tests were normal.
Clinical Course Prior to Diagnosis
Colonoscopy was performed. An abnormality seen in the cecum is shown in Figure 1.
Figure 1. Colonoscopic findings.
Diagnostic Procedure(s) and Result(s)
An intestinal worm was visualized on endoscopy, and identified as Trichuris trichiura (whipworm) based on its size.
Treatment and Followup
The patient was treated with mebendazole, with improvement.
Discussion
Final Diagnosis
Trichuriasis caused by infection with Trichuris trichiura (whipworm).
