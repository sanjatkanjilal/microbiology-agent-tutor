Final Diagnosis
Nocardia asteroides in sputum.
The gram stain shows thin, branching gram positive rods ( Figure 1 ), which are also seen on modified acid fast staining ( Figure 2 ). The organism was identified as Nocardia asteroides . Mixed flora are also present in the background of this specimen.
